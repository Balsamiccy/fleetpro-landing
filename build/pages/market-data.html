Comparable vehicles are matched using a tiered approach — exact specification first, then normalised generation, body style and variant — so a Roadster is never compared against a Coupe, and a Series 1 car is never priced against a Series 3.

  • Matched on make, model, generation, body style and variant — not just make and model
  • Sourced across auction houses and specialist dealers in the US, UK and Europe
  • Every comparable links back to its source listing, so you can check it yourself

These are market listings, not confirmed sold results, unless a specific record is known to have sold. Listing prices reflect what a vehicle was offered or estimated at, which may differ from its eventual sale price. See our full terms.
