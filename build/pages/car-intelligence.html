How it works

Matched on more
than make and model

We hold 438,000 market listings. When you add a car, Car Collector Studio matches it against comparable vehicles using a tiered approach — exact specification first, then normalised generation, body style and variant — so a Roadster is never compared against a Coupe, and a Series 1 car is never priced against a Series 3.

Your Collector Score then grades condition, originality, rarity, provenance and market demand, and the estimated market value is anchored to the median of the matched comparables — adjusted for that score. Every comparable links back to its source listing, so the reasoning behind a figure is never hidden.

Car Intelligence, explained

Car Collector Studio combines market comparables with vehicle-specific characteristics to provide a more informed view of value. It's a structured, data-driven process anchored to real comparable listings — not a black-box prediction — and every comparable is one you can open and check yourself.

Using a tiered approach: exact specification first, then normalised generation, body style and variant. A Roadster is never compared against a Coupe, and a Series 1 car is never priced against a Series 3.

Car Intelligence is a research and reference tool, not a certified appraisal. It gives you a documented, sourced starting point to discuss with an insurer, buyer or professional appraiser — not a substitute for one.
