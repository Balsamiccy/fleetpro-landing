About

Built for people who
understand the difference

Car Collector Studio exists for one reason: collector cars are not ordinary vehicles, and the tools used to manage them shouldn't treat them as if they were.

Why a fleet tool isn't the right tool

Most vehicle management software is built for fleets — delivery vans, company cars, rental inventory. It optimises for volume: dozens or hundreds of largely interchangeable vehicles, tracked for utilisation and maintenance cost. That's a reasonable thing to build, but it's the wrong shape for a collector's needs.

A collector car isn't interchangeable with another of the same make and model. A matching-numbers example is not the same asset as one with a replaced engine. A car with a complete, documented ownership history is not the same as one with a gap in its paper trail. Generation, body style, variant and factory specification can matter as much as the badge on the back. None of that fits a spreadsheet built for a delivery fleet.

What we built instead

Car Collector Studio brings two things together that are usually kept apart: careful, per-vehicle record-keeping — documents, service history, insurance, expenses, provenance — and market intelligence that respects the distinctions collectors actually care about, matched against comparable sales rather than averaged across a whole model line.

The result is a single private workspace for a collection: a place to keep the paperwork straight, understand what each car is actually worth relative to real comparable listings, and see the collection as a whole rather than one car at a time.

What the platform brings together

Explore the parts of the product that make this possible:

  • Collection Management — a complete, private record for every car you own.
  • Car Intelligence — comparables matched on specification, not just make and model.
  • Collector Score — a structured, five-factor way to understand a car's condition, originality, rarity, provenance and market demand.
  • Market Data — a growing dataset of comparable listings behind every valuation.
  • Pricing — plans for a single car up to a large, actively managed collection.

Where we're at

Car Collector Studio is an independently built, self-directed product, still growing its market-data coverage and its set of catalogued makes and models. We'd rather be straightforward about that than overstate scale we don't yet have — see our Market Overview for an honest picture of current data coverage.
