The five dimensions

What Collector Score
actually measures

Each dimension is graded individually, then combined into a single score that gives quick, consistent context — useful for comparing your own cars, or understanding how one compares to its matched listings.

Condition

The vehicle's mechanical and cosmetic state, from the details recorded in its record and documentation.

Originality

How much of the car remains as it left the factory — original components, finish and specification.

Rarity

How uncommon the specific generation, variant or special edition is within the wider market.

Provenance

Documented ownership history, notable past owners, and the completeness of the paper trail.

Market demand

Recent activity for comparable vehicles — how actively that generation and variant is being bought and sold.

Collector Score is a structured, consistent way to compare vehicles — it is not a precise mathematical formula or a certified appraisal, and it should be treated as a reference point rather than a guarantee of value or sale price. See our full terms.
