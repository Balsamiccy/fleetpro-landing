Car Intelligence

Know what your car
is worth — and why

Generic fleet software treats a 1973 Porsche 911 RS the same as a company van. Car Collector Studio doesn't. We hold 438,000 sold listings and match yours on generation, variant, body style and engine — not just make and model.

Your Collector Score grades condition, originality, rarity, provenance and market demand, then adjusts a market median anchored to real comparable sales. Every figure traces back to sales you can open and read — a valuation you can stand behind with an insurer, a buyer or an estate manager.

Market Intelligence

438,000+ market records
behind every decision

Explore comparable vehicles and understand the market context behind your collection — sold listings from auction houses and specialist dealers across the US, UK and Europe.

Example analysis · 1973 Porsche 911 RS

Comparable vehicles are matched using a tiered approach — exact specification first, then normalised generation, body style and variant — so a Roadster is never compared against a Coupe, and a Series 1 car is never priced against a Series 3.

  • Matched on make, model, generation, body style and variant — not just make and model
  • Sourced across auction houses and specialist dealers in the US, UK and Europe
  • Every comparable links back to its source listing, so you can check it yourself
