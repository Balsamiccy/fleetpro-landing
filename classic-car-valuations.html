Classic Car Valuations

classic car worth?

This is a market-based estimate, not a certified appraisal. It's a research and reference tool intended to give you a documented, sourced starting point — not a substitute for a professional appraisal where one is formally required. See our full terms.

How a valuation is built

From comparable listings
to an estimated value

01

Match on specification

Your car is matched against our database of 438,000+ market listings using a tiered approach — exact specification first, then normalised generation, body style and variant.

02

Score the vehicle

Condition, originality, rarity, provenance and market demand are graded to produce a Collector Score, giving context to where a specific car sits within its comparable set.

03

Anchor to the market

An estimated market value is anchored to the matched comparables and adjusted for the car's Collector Score — with every comparable listing left open for you to review.

Questions

Valuations, answered

No. Car Collector Studio's valuation is a market-based estimate built from comparable listings and vehicle characteristics. It is a research and reference tool, not a certified appraisal, and shouldn't be used as a substitute for one where a formal appraisal is required — for insurance underwriting, probate or a legal matter, for example.

A database of 438,000+ market listings, matched to your specific car on generation, variant, body style and engine, then combined with the car's own Collector Score to produce an estimated market value.

The estimate reflects the comparable listings and vehicle details in your account at the time you view it, so it updates as market data and your vehicle record change.

A basic specification is enough to get started. Adding more detail — condition, originality, provenance and documentation — sharpens the comparable match and the resulting Collector Score.
